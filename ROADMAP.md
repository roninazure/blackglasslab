# Swarm Edge Roadmap

1. **Phase 1: Stabilization and observability (COMPLETE)** - complete pipeline accounting, safe resolution lookup, reproducible tests, and a reconciled 25-market attended rotation.
2. **Phase 2: Statistical validation (IN PROGRESS)** - the read-only calibration baseline is complete; sample accumulation and statistically gated evaluation remain outstanding.
3. **Phase 3: Multi-model ensemble** - compare and combine independently evaluated forecasting models.
4. **Phase 4: Replay and backtesting** - reproduce historical decisions from timestamped market data.
5. **Phase 5: Terminal and dashboard** - expose validated operational and statistical reporting.
6. **Phase 6: Productionization** - harden deployment, monitoring, recovery, and operator controls.

Paper trading remains mandatory through the validation phases. Real-money execution is outside the current system.
