# Swarm Edge Roadmap

1. **Phase 1: Stabilization and observability** - complete.
2. **Phase 2: Statistical validation** - in progress; calibration baseline is complete, and temporal grounding safeguards are now in place. Sample accumulation and evidence gating continue.
3. **Phase 3: Loop Engine v1** - complete; deterministic opportunity ranking, bounded LLM budgets, category prompt routing, critic review, and latest-cycle brain reporting are implemented.
4. **Phase 3.1: Institutional market universe** - complete; formal default bans, market-quality gates, audited dry-run/apply rebuilds, category/horizon balancing, runtime enforcement, and policy telemetry are implemented.
5. **Phase 3.2: Institutional discovery expansion** - complete; event-aware category detection, tiered policy, multi-sort Gamma discovery, diversity caps, rejection analysis, and a 22-market CORE/RESEARCH watchlist are implemented.
6. **Phase 4: Calibration, replay, and backtesting** - calibrate score bands and critic outcomes, model correlated event exposure, then reproduce historical decisions from timestamped market data.
7. **Phase 5: Multi-model and live dashboard** - compare independent forecasters and stream validated brain activity into an operator terminal.
8. **Phase 6: Productionization** - harden deployment, monitoring, recovery, and operator controls.

Paper trading remains mandatory through the validation phases. Real-money execution is outside the current system.
