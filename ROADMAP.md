# Swarm Edge Roadmap

1. **Phase 1: Stabilization and observability** - complete.
2. **Phase 2: Statistical validation** - in progress; calibration baseline is complete, and temporal grounding safeguards are now in place. Sample accumulation and evidence gating continue.
3. **Phase 3: Loop Engine v1** - complete; deterministic opportunity ranking, bounded LLM budgets, category prompt routing, critic review, and latest-cycle brain reporting are implemented.
4. **Phase 4: Calibration, replay, and backtesting** - calibrate score bands and critic outcomes, then reproduce historical decisions from timestamped market data.
5. **Phase 5: Multi-model and live dashboard** - compare independent forecasters and stream validated brain activity into an operator terminal.
6. **Phase 6: Productionization** - harden deployment, monitoring, recovery, and operator controls.

Paper trading remains mandatory through the validation phases. Real-money execution is outside the current system.
